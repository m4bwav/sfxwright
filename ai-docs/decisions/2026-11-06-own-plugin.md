# Own plugin, not a musicwright sibling

Decided 2026-11-06 from the survey; the music boundary test is still owed and could move the line, not the decision.

Why: the routes differ (procedural, CC0 libraries, video-to-sound), the deliverable differs (variation sets and per-file provenance across hundreds of short files, UCS naming), and game and film plugins need it without any music install. Musicwright already scopes itself to music. Finishing reuses `mw.py` when present.

Name: sfxwright kept. It matches the sister plugins and the repo name was free on 2026-11-06.
