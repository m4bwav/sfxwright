## Models

- **Stable Audio 3 and Open 1.0:** Stability Community License. Business use requires registering; ends above USD 1M yearly revenue with affiliates. You own outputs as far as law allows. Whether earlier output survives termination: the text is silent. Do not bundle weights.
- **ElevenLabs:** free plan non-commercial per its use policy, though a product page differs; assume so. Paid is commercial; selling as a pack unverified.
- **Non-commercial weights:** AudioLDM 2, TangoFlux, AudioGen, Woosh, MMAudio, ControlFoley. Research or personal use only.
- **HunyuanVideo-Foley:** not valid in the EU, UK or South Korea; output may not improve other AI models.
- **Procedural:** no training data, no licence question.

## Recording your own

Bystanders' speech raises privacy, device and app sounds are usually someone's copyrighted design, and places that look public may be private land. Bigger productions may ask for a list of effects with proof of licence (`ship`). Fry (2019), UK practice, not legal advice.

## Copyright in output

US Copyright Office, Part 2, January 2025: purely AI-generated material is not protected, and prompts alone are not sufficient control. Human selection, arrangement and modification may be protected. So the edit trail in each take has value. How much human control suffices for a short effect is unsettled.

## Sound marks and copies

Reported as registered marks, secondary sources: NBC chimes, Intel bong, THX Deep Note, lightsaber, others. Check TSDR before relying on it. Rule here: do not recreate these or a named film's sounds; offer an original from traits. How far a soundalike reaches is unsettled.

## Stores

- **Steam:** disclose Pre-Generated AI content that ships and players consume, sound included; Live-Generated also needs guardrails.
- **itch.io:** tag generative AI; sound packs sold as game assets too. Self-contained algorithms without large external datasets need no disclosure, which favours procedural.
- **Epic:** no rule found. **Apple:** no AI label found in the part read; you need rights to content. **Google Play:** rules on runtime generation.
